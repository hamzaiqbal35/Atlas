In quantum field theory, a **vacuum** is not an empty void, but the state of lowest possible energy of all the quantum fields permeating space. A **true vacuum** is the absolute minimum energy state, ensuring the absolute stability of the universe's physical laws.

However, a **false vacuum** is a local minimum—a state that appears stable, but actually has a lower energy state existing elsewhere. 

## The Higgs Potential

The concept of a false vacuum in our universe is intimately tied to the **Higgs field**, which permeates all of space and gives elementary particles their mass. The energy landscape of the Higgs field is defined by a potential function $V(\phi)$.

According to the Standard Model of particle physics, the shape of this potential depends on the mass of the Higgs boson ($125 \text{ GeV}$) and the mass of the top quark ($173 \text{ GeV}$). 

When physicists plug these precise measured masses into the equations, the resulting potential curve is deeply unsettling: the current energy state of the Higgs field in our universe sits in a "valley" (a local minimum), but at much higher energy scales, the potential drops again into a much deeper valley (the true minimum).

## Metastability

Because we are stuck in the local minimum, our universe is considered **metastable**. It is stable enough to have lasted for 13.8 billion years, but it is not permanently stable. 

The barrier between our local minimum and the deeper minimum prevents the Higgs field from simply rolling down the hill. But in quantum mechanics, particles—and fields—do not always have to climb over barriers. They can tunnel through them.

## The Threat of Quantum Tunneling

If a quantum fluctuation allows the Higgs field in a tiny region of space to tunnel through the energy barrier and drop into the true vacuum, it would create a bubble of this new, lower-energy spacetime.

Because the true vacuum has a lower energy, this bubble would be incredibly stable and would immediately begin expanding outward at the speed of light, an apocalyptic event known as **vacuum decay**. Inside the bubble, the laws of physics, the masses of particles, and the nature of chemistry would be radically altered, instantly destroying all matter as we know it.

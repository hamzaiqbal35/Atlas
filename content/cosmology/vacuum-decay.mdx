**Vacuum decay** is the apocalyptic consequence of a false vacuum collapsing into a true vacuum. If our universe is currently occupying a metastable local minimum in the Higgs field potential, quantum mechanics dictates that it is only a matter of time before it transitions to the absolute minimum energy state.

## Bubble Nucleation

The transition would begin with a microscopic quantum fluctuation. Through a process called **quantum tunneling**, the Higgs field at a single, random point in space would bypass the energy barrier keeping it in the false vacuum state and drop into the true vacuum state.

This creates a microscopic "bubble" of true vacuum. Because the true vacuum has a lower energy density than the surrounding false vacuum of our universe, the bubble is energetically favored to grow. 

## Light-Speed Expansion

The energy difference between the false and true vacuums is released into the wall of the expanding bubble. This immense energy accelerates the bubble wall outward. Within a fraction of a second, the bubble wall approaches the speed of light ($c$).

Because the bubble expands at the speed of light, it is completely invisible and undetectable until the exact moment it strikes. No warning signal could ever precede it, because no signal travels faster than light.

## The Destruction of Physics

As the bubble wall passes through a region of space, the value of the Higgs field changes instantly. Because the Higgs field is responsible for giving elementary particles their mass, a change in the field fundamentally alters the masses of all particles.

Inside the true vacuum bubble:
- Electrons might become immensely heavy, or massless.
- The strong nuclear force might weaken, causing atomic nuclei to repel themselves and disintegrate.
- Complex chemistry, DNA, and planetary structures would become physically impossible.

Everything inside the bubble is instantly vaporized and converted into a soup of exotic particles governed by a completely alien set of physical laws. Sidney Coleman, one of the pioneers of this theory, famously described vacuum decay as "the ultimate ecological catastrophe."

## A Reassuring Note

While mathematically terrifying, calculations of the tunneling probability suggest that the expected lifetime of our metastable universe is exponentially longer than its current age—somewhere on the order of $10^{100}$ to $10^{500}$ years. Furthermore, if cosmic inflation occurred, our observable universe is likely safe for the foreseeable future.

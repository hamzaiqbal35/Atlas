## Overview
Electromagnetism is the physical interaction that occurs between electrically charged particles. It encompasses both electricity and magnetism, which are two aspects of a single fundamental force. This force is responsible for practically all the phenomena encountered in daily life, excluding gravity.

## The Electromagnetic Spectrum
Electromagnetic fields propagate through space as waves, carrying radiant energy. The electromagnetic spectrum covers a vast range of wavelengths and frequencies, from long radio waves to incredibly energetic gamma rays. Visible light is just a tiny fraction of this spectrum.

## Examples to Understand Concepts
- **The Balloon Analogy:** When you rub a balloon on your hair, you are transferring electrons, creating a static electric charge that can attract or repel other objects.
- **The Compass Needle:** A compass needle aligns itself with Earth's magnetic field, demonstrating how invisible magnetic forces act across distances.

## Recent Research & Discoveries
- Advanced research in quantum electrodynamics (QED) continues to probe the interactions of light and matter at unprecedented scales.
- Developments in metamaterials are creating new ways to manipulate electromagnetic waves, leading to technologies like cloaking devices and super-lenses.

## Overview
Quantum physics (or quantum mechanics) is the branch of physics that describes the behavior of matter and light on the atomic and subatomic scale. At this level, the classical laws of physics break down, and particles exhibit strange, probabilistic behaviors, existing in multiple states simultaneously and interacting instantaneously across vast distances.

## Wave-Particle Duality
One of the most fundamental concepts in quantum physics is wave-particle duality, which states that every particle or quantum entity can be described as either a particle or a wave. This was famously demonstrated by the double-slit experiment, where particles like electrons create an interference pattern when not observed, but act like distinct particles when measured.

## Examples to Understand Concepts
- **Schrödinger's Cat:** A famous thought experiment illustrating superposition. A cat in a sealed box with a radioactive mechanism is theoretically both alive and dead simultaneously until the box is opened and observed.
- **Quantum Entanglement:** If two particles become entangled, measuring the state of one instantly determines the state of the other, regardless of the distance separating them—a phenomenon Einstein called "spooky action at a distance."

## Recent Research & Discoveries
- Quantum computing is rapidly advancing, utilizing qubits in superposition to perform complex calculations exponentially faster than classical computers.
- Quantum cryptography and teleportation networks are being developed to create perfectly secure communication systems.

## Overview


![Irregular Galaxy](/irregular-inline.png)


An irregular galaxy is a galaxy that does not have a distinct regular shape, unlike a spiral or an elliptical galaxy.

## Specific Topic Details

They are often chaotic in appearance, with neither a nuclear bulge nor any trace of spiral arm structure. They are thought to make up about a quarter of all galaxies. Many irregulars were once spiral or elliptical galaxies but were deformed by gravitational pull from other galaxies. They often contain abundant amounts of gas and dust.

## Examples to Understand Concepts

- **The Cosmic Wreckage Analogy:** If regular galaxies are like well-designed buildings, irregular galaxies are the wreckage after a collision—chaotic, asymmetric, but full of raw materials for rebuilding (star formation).

## Recent Research & Discoveries

- Irregular galaxies, particularly dwarf irregulars, are studied as local analogs to the very early galaxies in the universe.
- Their high gas content makes them excellent laboratories for studying intense star formation under different conditions.

## Overview

The Local Group is the galaxy group that includes the Milky Way. It comprises more than 80 galaxies, most of which are dwarf galaxies.
## Specific Topic Details

Its gravitational center is located somewhere between the Milky Way and the Andromeda Galaxy. The Local Group covers a diameter of around 10 million light-years and has a binary dumbbell shape. The group itself is a part of the larger Virgo Supercluster, which in turn is a part of the Laniakea Supercluster.

## Examples to Understand Concepts

- **The Village Analogy:** If the Milky Way is our house, the Local Group is our small, quiet cosmic village, separated from other villages by vast expanses of empty space.

## Recent Research & Discoveries

- Astronomers continually discover new, extremely faint dwarf galaxies hiding in the Local Group.
- Observations are refining our understanding of the orbital dynamics between the Milky Way, Andromeda, and the Magellanic Clouds.

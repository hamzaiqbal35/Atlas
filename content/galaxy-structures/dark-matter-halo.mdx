## Overview

The dark matter halo is a theorized, massive, spherical envelope of dark matter that surrounds a galaxy and extends well beyond its visible boundaries.

![Dark Matter Halo](/dark-matter-halo-inline.png)

## Specific Topic Details

While completely invisible because it does not interact with light or electromagnetism, the dark matter halo contains the vast majority of a galaxy's mass. Its existence is inferred from its gravitational effects. Without the immense gravitational pull of the dark matter halo, the rapidly rotating visible disk of a spiral galaxy would fly apart.

## Examples to Understand Concepts

- **The Iceberg Analogy:** A galaxy is like an iceberg. The visible part—the stars, gas, and dust—is just the tip protruding above the water. The massive, invisible dark matter halo is the huge bulk of the iceberg hidden beneath the surface, holding everything together.

## Recent Research & Discoveries

- Scientists use gravitational lensing (measuring how the gravity of the halo bends light from background galaxies) to map the distribution of dark matter.
- Direct detection experiments and particle colliders are attempting to identify the elusive fundamental particle that makes up dark matter.

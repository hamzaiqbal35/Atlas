## Overview

A magnetar is a type of neutron star with an extremely powerful magnetic field, the strongest known in the universe.

![Magnetar](/magnetar-inline.png)

## Specific Topic Details

The magnetic field decay powers the emission of high-energy electromagnetic radiation, particularly X-rays and gamma rays. A magnetar's magnetic field is a thousand trillion times stronger than Earth's. If a magnetar were located halfway to the Moon, its magnetic field would be strong enough to wipe the magnetic stripes off every credit card on Earth.

## Examples to Understand Concepts

- **The Magnetic Monster Analogy:** The magnetic field is so intensely powerful that it actively stresses and stretches the solid crust of the neutron star. When the crust fractures under this strain (a "starquake"), it releases a flash of energy visible across the observable universe.

## Recent Research & Discoveries

- Magnetars are the leading candidates for the origin of Fast Radio Bursts (FRBs), mysterious millisecond-long flashes of radio energy from distant galaxies.
- Observations of Soft Gamma Repeaters (SGRs) and Anomalous X-ray Pulsars (AXPs) are used to study the behavior of these extreme magnetic fields.

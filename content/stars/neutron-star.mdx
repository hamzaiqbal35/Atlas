## Overview

A neutron star is the collapsed core of a massive supergiant star, which had a total mass of between 10 and 25 solar masses, that collapsed after a supernova explosion.

![Neutron Star](/neutron-star-inline.png)

## Specific Topic Details

They are the smallest and densest currently known class of stellar objects. A neutron star packs more mass than our Sun into a sphere the size of a city. The gravity is so intense that electrons and protons are crushed together to form neutrons. A single teaspoon of neutron star material would weigh billions of tons on Earth.

## Examples to Understand Concepts

- **The Extreme Compression Analogy:** Imagine taking the mass of the entire Earth, and then compressing it down into a space no bigger than a single sugar cube. That is the kind of extreme density found inside a neutron star.

## Recent Research & Discoveries

- LIGO and Virgo observatories detect gravitational waves from merging neutron stars, which are thought to be the source of the heaviest elements in the universe, like gold and platinum.
- The exact "equation of state" (the internal structure and behavior of matter under such extreme pressure) remains one of the biggest unsolved mysteries in astrophysics.

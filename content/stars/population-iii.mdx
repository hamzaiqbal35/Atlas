## Overview

Population III stars are a hypothetical population of extremely massive, hot, and luminous stars that were the first to form in the universe after the Big Bang.

![Population III](/population-iii-inline.png)

## Specific Topic Details

They would have had virtually zero metallicity, formed exclusively from primordial hydrogen, helium, and trace amounts of lithium. Without heavy elements to help cool the collapsing gas clouds, these stars are theorized to have been incredibly massive (hundreds of solar masses) and short-lived. They ended their lives in massive supernovae, seeding the cosmos with the first heavy elements.

## Examples to Understand Concepts

- **The Founders Analogy:** They are the legendary founders of the cosmic city. They arrived when there was nothing but raw land (hydrogen/helium), built the first massive, crude structures, and upon their destruction, left behind the refined materials that all subsequent generations used to build the modern universe.

## Recent Research & Discoveries

- The James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) was specifically designed to peer back far enough in time to detect the faint signatures of the very first galaxies, which might contain Population III stars.
- Astronomers search for "chemical signatures" in extremely old Population II stars that would match the expected supernova yields of Population III stars.

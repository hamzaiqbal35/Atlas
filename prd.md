# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Atlas - The Ultimate Space Encyclopedia

## 1. Vision & Concept
**Atlas** is an "Interactive Knowledge Experience" for space exploration. It acts as the ultimate digital space encyclopedia, moving far beyond a simple solar system map. It is designed to model the universe exactly how NASA, ESA, astronomers, and astrophysicists mentally organize it. Users traverse a 3D environment and a rich knowledge graph where information emerges naturally based on their perspective, scale, and location.

## 2. Target Audience
- Users seeking premium, highly polished digital experiences.
- Astronomy, astrophysics, and space enthusiasts.
- Design and engineering professionals (as a portfolio piece).
- Educators and students looking for a comprehensive, scientifically accurate reference.

## 3. Core Principles
- **UX is the Cake, Animation is the Icing:** Information architecture, typography, spacing, accessibility, and performance take precedence over flashy effects.
- **Premium Aesthetics:** Classy, modern, professional. Think Apple, Stripe, Linear, Vercel, Notion, Figma, Raycast. Not Crypto landing pages, AI startup templates, or Themeforest demos.
- **Restraint:** Neutral colors, subtle animations, and purposeful interaction design. No neon gradients, purple everywhere, or glassmorphism on every card.

## 4. Key Features
- **The Ultimate Taxonomy:** Content spans 40 interconnected domains ranging from Fundamental Physics, Elementary Particles, and Chemistry, up through Stars, Galaxies, Cosmic Structures, to Human Spaceflight and Space Technology.
- **Interactive 3D Universe:** Built with React Three Fiber. Users can click on objects to fly the camera there, triggering smooth rotations and orbit animations.
- **Knowledge Graph Traversal:** Users navigate through topics relationally (e.g., from a Black Hole to General Relativity to Spacetime) rather than traditional hierarchical menus.
- **Floating UI:** Information cards, timelines, and comparisons emerge physically and gently into the view.
- **The Observatory Dashboard:** A sleek, Bloomberg Terminal-meets-Apple style dashboard providing real-world data and telemetry.
- **Mini Map & Universal Search:** Breadcrumbs, instant search (Cmd/Ctrl+K) across all 40 domains, and a global view.
- **Perfect Dual Themes:** Flawless execution of both Dark (Deep Space) and Light (Daylight Observatory) modes.
